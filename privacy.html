When you reserve a spot for demo day, you give us your name and mobile number. Here's exactly what we do with them.

Who we are

Tambora Technologies (Pty) Ltd, a South African company. We're responsible for your details under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Your full name
  • Your mobile number
  • That you agreed to be contacted about demo day

Your browser also remembers your first name on your own device, so the page knows you've already signed up. That never leaves your phone or computer.

Why we use it

Only to send you your demo day invite and messages about the event, like the venue, time and any changes. We won't use it for anything else, and we'll never sell it or share it with other companies for marketing.

Where it's kept

Your details are stored in a Google Form and spreadsheet that only Tambora's founders can open. Google may store this data on servers outside South Africa, protected by Google's own security.

How long we keep it

Until demo day is over. After that we delete it, unless you tell us you'd like to keep hearing from us.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the details we hold, correct them, or delete them at any time. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to South Africa's Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Remove my number

Reply STOP to any message we send you, and we'll delete your name and number from our list.
