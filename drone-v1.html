Drone V1 takes off.

A new kind of first responder is on duty.

Cape Town, South Africa. Tambora today introduced Drone V1, its first autonomous security drone, built together with SafeGuard Technologies. It lives on a rooftop dock and launches the moment an alarm goes off, so it's overhead in the time it takes most people to find their keys. It flies itself, reads the ground below, and can speak to whoever's there. When the job is done, it lands on its dock and charges itself.

No pilot. No waiting. It guards a site around the clock, and it's the first step in Tambora's mission to bring autonomy to essential services.

Built with SafeGuard.

Drone V1 was designed and built by the SafeGuard team, working inside Tambora. SafeGuard is co-owned with Tambora and brings the on-the-ground security know-how. Tambora builds the aircraft, the dock and the software. SafeGuard takes it to the people who need it and keeps it flying on site.

It's a partnership built so that every security call teaches the system something, and every lesson makes the next drone better.

"Guards can't be everywhere at once. Drone V1 can be there first, see what's happening and tell the team exactly what they're walking into."Tumelo Mokoena, Director of SafeGuard and Tambora co-founder [ draft quote, Tumelo to approve ]

Home is a dock.

Drone V1 lives in a weatherproof dock on a rooftop near the site it protects. The dock opens, launches the drone, brings it home and charges it, all on its own. Nobody needs to touch it between flights.

When the alarm goes off.

  1. 01

    Alarm.

    A security alarm or panic button sends the alert to Tambora.

  2. 02

    Launch.

    Drone V1 lifts off from its dock and flies itself to the scene.

  3. 03

    Eyes on it.

    The guard watches live on their phone, and the drone can speak to whoever's on the ground.

Reads the ground.

Tambora's software tells people from cars, trees and everything else, then finds what it's looking for, even in a crowd. Guards see what matters, not just a video feed.
