One company, one mission

One company.
Built in increments.

Tambora is the parent company. It owns the technology, the AI and the team.

Every step of the business runs on the same aircraft, docks and software, so each new mission starts with everything the last one proved.

Most autonomy is built for consumers. Ours is built for the services that protect them.

// meanwhile, in the lab

Every flight starts
on the bench.

Sagwic is Tambora's research lab. It designs and builds the docks, and develops the AI layer on open-source models. Everything Tambora flies is worked out here first.

  • docks that swap batteries by themselves
  • batteries that last longer in the heat
  • autonomy you don't have to pilot
  • payloads that get heavier every version
