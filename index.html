Tambora

Autonomous drones,
docks and AI.

Tambora builds autonomous drones, the docks they live in and the AI that flies them. We start with security in South Africa, with one simple aircraft and a clear focus on getting the AI and the data right. The later steps move into medical and heavy-load aviation, and eventually vehicles that fly.

One company, one mission

One company.
Built in increments.

Tambora is the parent company. It owns the technology, the AI and the team.

Every step of the business runs on the same aircraft, docks and software, so each new mission starts with everything the last one proved.

The phases

From simple
to complex.

We start with the simplest aircraft and the simplest job, then move up one step at a time. Each version flies before the next one is built.
