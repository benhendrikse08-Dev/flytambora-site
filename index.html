TAMBORA-AI · The brain

You'll never see it.
That's the point.

No one looks at the brain when someone saves their life. They see the paramedic arrive, the fire put out, the guard who got there in time. TamboraAI works the same way. It's never the face of what we do. It's the reason any of it works.

What it does

TamboraAI is the intelligence that runs every Tambora drone. It processes live video from the air and turns it into decisions, in real time.

It detects what's on the ground: vehicles, people, smoke, fire and changes in a scene. It understands its surroundings, mapping the area it patrols and learning what normal looks like, so unusual activity stands out immediately. It reacts to what it finds: it flags incidents, assesses how serious they are, holds the drone over the scene and alerts the right responders with a live feed. And it navigates safely, flying its route, avoiding obstacles and returning to its dock on its own.

One brain, every service

We're building TamboraAI as one brain for every service we run. Medical, security and firefighting each ask different questions of the ground below: is someone where they shouldn't be, has a car stopped in a live lane, is that haze or smoke? Underneath, the skills are the same: see clearly, understand the scene and act quickly.

That's why one platform makes sense. What the brain learns watching a highway at night can sharpen how it reads a building site at night, and every new service makes the whole network more capable.

Built for South Africa

TamboraAI is trained for the places it will actually fly: our highways, our townships and suburbs, our light, our weather and our fire seasons. A model trained somewhere else doesn't know what a taxi rank at 6 a.m. looks like, or how a veld fire moves in the wind.

Every patrol adds to that knowledge. The more our drones fly, the better the brain understands the areas it serves.

Built to be trusted

A brain this capable has to know its limits. Every Tambora flight is supervised by licensed remote pilots who can take control at any moment, and any decision that carries real risk is handed to a person. TamboraAI is there to give responders better information, faster. It isn't there to replace them.

It's also built to respect the people below. Anyone and anything that isn't part of an incident is blurred, footage is only shared with the people who need it to respond, and the way we handle data is designed around South Africa's privacy law, POPIA. Trust is earned one flight at a time, and we intend to earn it.
